# Data Availability

This v1.0.0 release (2026-08-14) contains the reviewed aggregate CSV carriers,
figure source data, dictionaries, and source metadata needed for the offline
Level 1 reproduction. The public-source register identifies the external
reports, regulations, articles, and engineering references; their raw payloads
are not redistributed and remain subject to the source-specific rights stated
in the register.

The four controlled families in `data/controlled_inputs_metadata.csv` are
metadata-only. Exact directed topology, facility mappings, candidate-link
geometry, and GS(2023)2767 map files require owner approval or formal rights
review and have no public download route in this release. Their hashes are
`hash_unavailable` with a reason because the restricted payloads are absent.

Author-generated aggregate values are explicitly labelled in the registers and
aggregate dictionaries. Those aggregate carriers are released under CC BY 4.0
with attribution; this grant does not extend to public-source payloads,
controlled metadata, or any third-party material. No DOI or accession number is
assigned by this GitHub release.

The manuscript authority and its digest are recorded in
[MANUSCRIPT_SCOPE.md](MANUSCRIPT_SCOPE.md), but the DOCX itself is not included.
